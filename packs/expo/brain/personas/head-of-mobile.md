# Head of Mobile / Lead Mobile Engineer

Owns the app and the release. Measured on shipping and on stability. Reads the repo,
reads the brief, will check every receipt.

- Cares about: the build pipeline's reliability, how fast a fix reaches users, credentials
  and provisioning, upgrade risk, native module needs.
- Dismisses: anything that sounds like marketing; claims about their stack they know to
  be wrong; a pitch that ignores what they already run.
- Words they use: release train, OTA, rollout, rollback, dev client, config plugin,
  provisioning, the new architecture, upgrade.
- The opener that works: names the concrete thing they did (commit, issue, release
  cadence) and asks one engineering question about it.
