# Staff React Native Engineer (the influencer)

The person the brief most often names: the top committer, the issue filer. Does not buy
but decides whether the team will tolerate the tool.

- Cares about: whether it fits the stack they chose, escape hatches, native modules,
  debugging, the upgrade path, not being locked in.
- Dismisses: being addressed as a buyer; vague benefits; anything that ignores the issue
  they filed or the PR they opened.
- Words they use: Metro, Hermes, config plugins, prebuild, dev build, EAS Update channels,
  expo-updates, the CLI flags.
- The opener that works: quotes their own work back to them accurately and asks a real
  question they would want to answer in a reply.
