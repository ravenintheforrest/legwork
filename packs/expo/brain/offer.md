# What we offer

- **EAS Build** — cloud builds for iOS and Android, credentials managed, development
  builds for the team, no Xcode on anyone's laptop.
- **EAS Update** — over-the-air JavaScript updates: hotfixes in minutes, staged rollouts,
  rollbacks, channels per environment.
- **EAS Submit** — store submission from the command line or CI.
- **EAS Hosting / Workflows** — hosting for the web side and the API routes, and workflows
  that chain build → update → submit on a trigger.
- **Enterprise** — SSO/SAML, priority support, dedicated infrastructure, compliance
  paperwork, and the people to run a migration with you.

## What counts as a good fit
A shipped app that matters to the business, a JS-first team, and a reason now: hiring,
a release cadence, an upgrade, a compliance need. A hobbyist's side project is not a fit
and is never an account. A company with no app and no hiring is not a fit yet.

## Claims to avoid
- Any price, headcount, funding, or revenue not in the evidence array.
- "Faster than X" without a cited metric.
- Anything about a person that their public profile does not say.
- Promises about roadmap.
