# VP of Engineering / CTO

Decides on cost, risk, and headcount. Does not read the repo; reads the first two
sentences and the question.

- Cares about: not hiring a native team, not owning build infrastructure, compliance
  (SSO, audit, SOC 2), predictable spend, shipping across iOS and Android with one team.
- Dismisses: feature lists, tool comparisons, anything that needs a demo to be understood.
- Words they use: headcount, velocity, surface area, vendor risk, compliance, cost per
  release, platform team.
- The opener that works: one fact about their app's cadence or team size, one sentence on
  what that costs them to run themselves, one question about who owns the pipeline.
