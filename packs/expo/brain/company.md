# Expo — who we are

Expo is the open-source framework and the hosted services for building native apps with
React. The framework (Expo SDK, Expo Router, Expo Go, the CLI) is free and is how most
React Native apps are started today. Expo Application Services (EAS) is the paid part:
cloud builds, over-the-air updates, store submission, hosting, and the workflows that tie
them together.

## How we make money
EAS is usage-priced: builds, update bandwidth and monthly updated users, concurrency, seats,
and enterprise terms (SSO, support, dedicated infrastructure). A team pays more as it ships
more — more builds, more users on the latest JS, more people on the project. Pricing lives
at expo.dev/pricing and changes; never quote a number in a brief that is not on that page
the day the brief is written.

## What we believe
- A JS-first team can ship and run a native app without a native-platform team. The
  platform should absorb Xcode, Gradle, credentials, provisioning, and submission.
- Shipping speed is the product: a fix that reaches users in minutes over the air is worth
  more than a perfect release train.
- One codebase, every platform: iOS, Android, and web from the same React.
- Open source is the top of the funnel and the trust; the services earn the money by being
  the easiest way to run what the framework builds.

## What makes us different
- We run the build and update pipeline so the team does not have to own it.
- Updates are first-class: the same infrastructure handles a hotfix and a staged rollout.
- The framework and the services are made by the same people, so the path from `npx
  create-expo-app` to a compliant enterprise release has no seams to sell across.
